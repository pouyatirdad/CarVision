      Privacy

      Your images never leave your device. All image recognition is performed locally in your browser.

      • No server, no account, no tracking.
      • Images are not stored and not sent anywhere.
      • The AI models are downloaded once and cached by your browser.

      Models: YOLOX-tiny detector (Apache-2.0, Megvii) · CLIP ViT-B/32 image encoder (MIT, OpenCLIP, DataComp-1B weights) with a rear-view head trained for CarVision · optional older general-view classifiers: ResNet18 Iranian car classifier (MIT, RezaGooner) · ViT-B/16 Stanford Cars classifier (Apache-2.0, therealcyberlord) · ONNX Runtime Web (MIT).

      Predictions are statistical estimates and can be wrong. Never rely on them for anything important.
